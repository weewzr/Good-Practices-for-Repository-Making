# Architecture and Scientific Decision Records

## Purpose

Consequential choices should not disappear into chat history.

Use a short decision record when a choice materially affects:
- system boundary;
- model architecture;
- numerical method;
- solver;
- reference design;
- source selection;
- external integration;
- data format;
- workflow/reproducibility architecture.

## Rule

Decision records explain why a path was chosen. They do not convert judgment or assumptions into facts.
