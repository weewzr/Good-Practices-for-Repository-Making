# Negative Results and Research Log Policy

## Why preserve negative results?

Research repositories should capture:
- what was attempted;
- what happened;
- why it was accepted or rejected;
- what was learned;
- what decision followed.

This prevents repeated dead ends and retrospective rewriting of the research story.

## Suggested structure

```
research_log/
  INDEX.md
  EXP-001.md
  EXP-002.md
  ...
```

Each entry should be short and evidence-linked.

## Entry template

ID:

Date:

Question:

Method/configuration:

Result:

Verification:

Interpretation:

Decision:

Canonical impact: NONE / CANDIDATE / PROMOTED / SUPERSEDED

Artifacts:

## Do not use the log as a diary

Record scientifically consequential work, not every chat message or command.

The log is a decision/evidence trail.
