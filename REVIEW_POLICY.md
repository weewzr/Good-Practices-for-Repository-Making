# Review and Verification Policy

## Principle

Independent review is most valuable after a well-defined scientific object exists.

## Reviewer roles

### Scientific reviewer
Checks:
- assumptions;
- equations;
- physical interpretation;
- evidence quality;
- boundaries;
- overclaiming.

### Numerical reviewer
Checks:
- implementation;
- convergence;
- stability;
- conservation;
- stochastic uncertainty;
- reproducibility.

### Manuscript reviewer
Checks:
- logical sequence;
- claim/evidence alignment;
- figure/table clarity;
- citation completeness;
- writing quality;
- whether conclusions exceed evidence.

One reviewer may cover several roles, but the criteria should remain explicit.

## Severity

Use:
- BLOCKER;
- MAJOR;
- MODERATE;
- MINOR;
- NOTE.

Do not turn severity into an overall score or ranking.

## Closure

Every finding requires:
1. exact finding;
2. proposed resolution;
3. files/equations/tests affected;
4. post-resolution evidence;
5. closure status.

Historical review documents remain immutable once issued.
